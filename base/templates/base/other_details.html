Returns & Exchange Policy

Everything you need to know about our policies and procedures

Important Policy Notice

You have 5 days from the delivery date for exchanges only. Returns are not accepted.

Exchange Policy

Please review our exchange guidelines carefully before making a purchase:

Items must be unused and in original packaging

Exchange requests must be initiated within 5 days of delivery

Shipping costs for exchanges are the customer's responsibility

Exchanges are processed within 3-5 business days

To initiate an exchange, please contact our customer support team at support@zevaristan.com with your order details.

Exchange Process

1

Request Exchange

Contact our support team within 5 days of delivery with your order number and exchange details.

2

Receive Approval

Our team will review your request and send you exchange instructions and a return label via email.

3

Ship Your Item

Package your item securely in its original packaging and ship it back using the provided label.

4

Receive Your Exchange

Once we receive your item, we'll process your exchange and ship the new item within 3-5 business days.
